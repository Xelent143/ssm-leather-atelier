# Create and Publish Your First Product

This is the Owner operating procedure for Product Editor v2. It uses the current MOTOGRIP OS governance workflow and does not bypass Product Identity, Product Releases, or Knowledge Locks.

## Before you begin

- Sign in as the Named Owner.
- Prepare the product name, category, selling price, and at least one factual product image.
- Confirm that the image and product information belong to the same physical design.

## 1. Create the draft

1. Open **Commerce → Add Product**.
2. Leave **Manual Entry** selected, or use **Analyze with AI** when image analysis is useful.
3. Complete the five **Quick listing readiness** fields:
   - Product name
   - Category
   - Price
   - Primary image
   - Product status
4. Select **Save Draft**.

The URL handle is derived safely from the product name. Brand and product-type defaults may be corrected before approval. Description, SEO, Merchant attributes, variants, metafields, and other information can remain incomplete or **Needs Confirmation** during a quick listing.

## 2. Add factual information

1. Upload additional images and assign their roles.
2. Add variants, inventory, shipping information, and factual metafields when available.
3. Optionally run **AI Vision** and review every observation.
4. Optionally use **AI Product Copilot**.
5. Accept only facts supported by Product DNA, approved evidence, or direct Owner confirmation.

AI suggestions never become Product DNA facts automatically.

## 3. Review website content

The public product page always uses this order:

1. Description
2. Features
3. Specifications
4. Perfect For
5. Why You'll Love It

Empty sections remain hidden. Do not add unsupported material, protection, certification, hardware-brand, waterproofing, or performance claims.

## 4. Preview the draft

1. Select **Preview Website**.
2. Confirm the private preview banner is visible.
3. Verify the title, price, images, options, availability, and completed content sections.
4. Return to Product Editor and correct any issue.

Draft previews are authenticated, private, and excluded from search indexing.

## 5. Complete governance

1. Select **Submit for Review**.
2. As Owner, select **Approve**.
3. Open the product's **Product Detail** workspace.
4. Generate, approve, and lock **Product Identity**.
5. Create a **Product Version**.
6. Create the **Approval Request** and record Owner approval.
7. Create and activate the **Product Release**.
8. Create the **Knowledge Lock**.

These controls protect the permanent identity and ensure publication consumes approved product knowledge.

## 6. Publish

1. Return to Product Editor v2.
2. Confirm **Quick listing fields complete**.
3. Confirm Product Identity, Product Release, Knowledge Lock, and Owner approval are valid.
4. Select **Approve & Publish**.
5. Open the public product page and verify the storefront.
6. Confirm the Product Grid and Category views show the synchronized product.

Publishing updates the existing website identity. It does not create a duplicate when the product is republished.

## 7. Edit and republish

1. Open the Live product in Product Editor v2.
2. Select **Create New Revision**.
3. Edit and save the draft.
4. Repeat review, approval, Release, and Knowledge Lock steps for the new revision.
5. Republish and verify the same product URL, Product UUID, and SKU remain in use.

## 8. Hide and restore

1. From **Product Management**, use **Hide / Unlist**.
2. Confirm the Product Grid shows **Hidden** and the public product is unavailable.
3. Use **Restore** to reactivate the same product.
4. Confirm the URL, Product UUID, Product SKU, variant SKUs, media, and category assignments remain unchanged.

## Final Owner checklist

- Product identity belongs to the correct design.
- Primary image is accurate and featured.
- Price and availability are correct.
- Public content contains no unsupported claims.
- Website sections appear in the permanent five-section order.
- Product Identity is locked.
- Approved Product Release and Knowledge Lock exist.
- Draft preview is correct.
- Public page, Product Grid, Category, and search projection agree after publication.
